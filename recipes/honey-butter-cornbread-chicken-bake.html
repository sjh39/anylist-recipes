Honey Butter Cornbread Chicken Bake

A sweet, savory, cheesy chicken and creamed corn filling topped with golden, buttery honey cornbread.

Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

Chicken filling

  • 19.8 oz raw chicken tenderloins
  • 1 can (14.75 oz) cream style sweet corn
  • 1 can (10.5 oz) 98% fat free cream of chicken soup
  • 3 oz reduced fat cream cheese
  • 3/4 cup fat free shredded cheddar
  • 1 tsp garlic butter seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Cornbread topping

  • 3.7 oz Krusteaz Honey Cornbread Mix (1/4 bag)
  • 1.4 fl oz fat free Fairlife milk
  • 0.7 oz unsalted butter, melted
  • 0.4 oz beaten egg (1/4 large egg)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease baking dish.
  2. Cook chicken to 165°F and shred.
  3. Mix soup, softened cream cheese, corn and seasonings. Stir in hot chicken and cheddar.
  4. Spread filling into baking dish.
  5. Mix cornbread ingredients until just combined.
  6. Spoon batter over filling in 6 dollops. Gently spread each into a thin layer. Gaps in between are fine.
  7. Bake uncovered 30-40 min until golden, cornbread is cooked through and filling reaches 165°F. Cover with foil if browning too quickly.
  8. Rest 5-10 min, divide into 6 and enjoy.
