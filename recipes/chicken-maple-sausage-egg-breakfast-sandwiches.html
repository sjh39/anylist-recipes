Chicken Maple Sausage and Egg Breakfast Sandwiches

Source: @gatherednutrition

High-protein freezer breakfast sandwiches: an oven-baked spinach and cottage cheese egg bake, pan-cooked chicken maple sausage patties, and spicy cheddar on English muffins. Made ahead, wrapped, frozen, and reheated in the microwave or oven.

Yield: 10 sandwiches

Ingredients

Egg Bake

  • 12 large eggs
  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Chicken Maple Sausage Patties

  • 2 lb ground chicken
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp poultry seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp dried sage
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Red pepper flakes, to taste

Assembly

  • 10 English muffins
  • 10 slices spicy cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and line a 9x13-inch baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. Blend the eggs, cottage cheese, spinach, 1/2 tsp sea salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper until smooth.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the prepared pan and bake for 17-20 minutes, until the eggs are cooked through. Set aside for assembly.
  4. Mix the ground chicken, maple syrup, olive oil, cinnamon, poultry seasoning, sage, garlic powder, 2 tsp sea salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper, and red pepper flakes until well combined. Divide into 10 equal portions.
  5. Heat a large pan over medium heat. Add the patties to the hot pan, pressing them down firmly since they shrink while cooking. Cook 3-4 minutes until browned.
  6. Flip the patties and cook 2-3 more minutes until cooked through. Set aside for assembly.
  7. Cut the egg bake into circles or squares. Save any egg scraps to add to the sandwiches.
  8. Slice the English muffins. Add an egg patty, a sausage patty, and a slice of spicy cheddar to each.
  9. Wrap each sandwich in parchment paper or foil and freeze until ready to heat.
  10. To reheat, thaw a sandwich in the refrigerator the night before.
  11. Microwave: wrap the thawed sandwich in a damp paper towel and microwave about 2 minutes, until the eggs and sausage are heated through.
  12. Oven: preheat to 350°F and let the thawed sandwich sit on the counter while the oven heats. Place on a baking sheet and bake until heated through.
