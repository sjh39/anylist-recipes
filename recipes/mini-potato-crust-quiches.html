Mini Potato Crust Quiches

Crispy golden potato crusts filled with fluffy eggs, creamy ricotta, bacon, and cheddar cheese. Makes 12 mini quiches, 2 per serving. A breakfast to prep once and eat all week.

Yield: 6 servings (12 mini quiches, 2 per serving)

Ingredients

  • 6 medium-large Yukon gold potatoes
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup ricotta cheese
  • 7 slices cooked bacon, chopped
  • Heaping 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Green onions or scallions, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil spray

Instructions

  1. Cut the potatoes in half and boil until fork-tender. Allow them to cool slightly, then peel the skins off if desired (you can leave the skins on, too).
  2. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Spray a muffin tin generously with avocado oil. Place one potato half into each muffin cup. Using the bottom of a small spice jar or glass (sprayed with oil so it does not stick), press each potato down to form a mini crust.
  3. Spray the potato crusts again with avocado oil and season with salt. Par-bake at 400°F for 30 to 35 minutes, until the edges begin to turn golden and crispy.
  4. While the crusts bake, whisk together the eggs, ricotta, chopped bacon, cheddar cheese, and some of the chopped green onions. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Remove the potato crusts from the oven and carefully pour the egg mixture into each cup, filling close to the top. You should have just enough mixture for all 12.
  6. Return to the oven and bake at 400°F for another 15 minutes, or until the eggs are completely set. Insert a toothpick into the center to make sure they are cooked through.
  7. Allow to cool slightly before removing from the muffin tin. Finish with flaky salt and additional chopped green onions.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (2 mini quiches, USDA FoodData Central): 372 calories | 18 g protein | 36 g carbohydrates | 17 g fat | 5 g fiber
