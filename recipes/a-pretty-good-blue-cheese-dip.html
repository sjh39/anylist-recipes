A Pretty Good Blue Cheese Dip

Source: Alison Roman

A garlicky sour cream dip with lemon and scallions, finished with crumbled blue cheese. Serve it as a dip for cold crunchy vegetables or use it as a dressing. Amounts use the midpoints of the source ranges.

Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 2 scallions, finely chopped
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 9 oz sour cream or full-fat Greek yogurt
  • 5 oz blue cheese, crumbled

Instructions

  1. Grate the garlic into a medium bowl.
  2. Add the scallions, squeeze the juice of the lemon over the top, pick out any seeds, and season with salt and black pepper.
  3. Add the 9 oz sour cream or Greek yogurt and season again with salt and black pepper. Taste and add more lemon juice if you want it tangier.
  4. Crumble the 5 oz blue cheese into the bowl by hand and stir to combine.
  5. If the dip is too thick, thin it with a splash of buttermilk, lemon juice, or water (optional).

Notes

Alison Roman's notes (paraphrased):

  • Without the blue cheese, the base is still a good all-purpose garlicky dip.
  • Her pick is Bayley Hazen Blue from Jasper Hill Farm. She prefers a creamier blue cheese that you crumble yourself, because pre-crumbled blue cheese is dry and does not mix in well.
  • The finished dip tastes like ranch with blue cheese added, not like a classic blue cheese dressing.
  • Uses: wedge salad dressing, BLT spread, dip for cold crunchy vegetables, dressing for a cucumber salad with dill, or dressing for chicken over romaine with a jammy egg.
