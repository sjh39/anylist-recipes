Chicken & Mushroom Takikomi Gohan Onigiri

Source: @rikaseats

One-pot Japanese seasoned rice (takikomi gohan) with chicken thigh, shiitake mushrooms, carrot, and edamame, cooked together in a rice cooker and shaped into riceballs (onigiri).

Yield: 8 onigiri

Ingredients

Rice and Mix-ins

  • 360 ml Japanese short-grain rice
  • 200 g boneless chicken thigh, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1/4 cup frozen edamame
  • 1/4 carrot, julienned
  • 1/2 pack shiitake mushrooms, sliced

Seasoning

  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sake
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 2 tsp hondashi
  • Water, as directed by rice cooker

Instructions

  1. Add the rice to the rice cooker pot.
  2. Add the salt, soy sauce, sake, mirin, and hondashi to the rice.
  3. After adding the seasonings, add water to the level directed by the rice cooker for the amount of rice.
  4. Layer the chicken, shiitake mushrooms, carrot, and edamame on top of the rice without stirring.
  5. Cook on the white rice setting.
  6. When cooking is finished, mix everything together.
  7. Shape the rice into 8 onigiri.
