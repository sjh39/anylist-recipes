BBQ Chicken Loaded Baked Potato Casserole

Oven-baked BBQ chicken thighs layered over cottage cheese mashed potatoes, then topped with red onion, green onions, and sharp cheddar. Makes 10 servings for meal prep. Reheat and top with 0% Greek yogurt or sour cream if desired.

Yield: 10 servings

Ingredients

BBQ Chicken

  • 1800 g boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 Tbsp Morton kosher salt
  • 3/4 cup BBQ sauce
  • 45 g tomato paste
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp smoked paprika

Mashed Potatoes

  • 2250 g Yukon gold potatoes, unpeeled, cut into chunks
  • 450 g 2% cottage cheese
  • Rendered chicken juices (from baking the chicken)
  • 85 g bacon bits

Loaded Toppings

  • 200 g red onion, diced
  • 1 bunch green onions, sliced
  • 227 g full-fat sharp cheddar, shredded

Optional, After Reheating

  • 0% Greek yogurt or sour cream, for topping (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix 1800 g boneless skinless chicken thighs, 2 Tbsp Morton kosher salt, 3/4 cup BBQ sauce, 45 g tomato paste, 1 Tbsp olive oil, 1 Tbsp black pepper, and 1 Tbsp smoked paprika until coated. Pack tightly into 2 foil loaf pans, about 2 thighs per layer. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour, ideally 12+ hours.
  2. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Uncover the pans, set them on a sheet pan, and bake 55-60 minutes, until the center reaches at least 175°F. Rest the chicken in the pans 15-20 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, add 2250 g Yukon gold potatoes to a 7-qt+ pot and cover with cold salted water. Bring to a boil over high heat, then boil 20-25 minutes, until fork-tender. Drain, return to the pot, and mash.
  4. In a blender, add 450 g 2% cottage cheese. Pour in the rendered chicken juices from both pans and blend until smooth. Add to the potatoes and mash until thick. Stir in 85 g bacon bits.
  5. Set a 15" x 11" x 3" foil roaster pan on a sheet pan and spread the potatoes in it. Sprinkle with a third of the 200 g red onion and 1 bunch green onions.
  6. Finely chop the chicken and spread it on top. Add the remaining onions, then 227 g full-fat sharp cheddar.
  7. Bake 20 minutes, then rest 10 minutes. Divide into 10 servings. After reheating, top with 0% Greek yogurt or sour cream if desired.

Nutrition

Per serving (10 servings): 580 calories | 55 g protein | 48 g carbohydrates | 19 g fat | 5 g fiber (fiber estimated from USDA FoodData Central)
