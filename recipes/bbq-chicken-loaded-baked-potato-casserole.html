BBQ Chicken Loaded Baked Potato Casserole

BIG BATCH Bakes, Episode 1. A high-protein meal prep casserole: oven-baked BBQ chicken thighs layered over mashed Yukon Gold potatoes blended with a cottage cheese and chicken-juice cream sauce, bacon bits, red and green onions, and sharp cheddar. Bakes in the oven and reheats from frozen in the microwave.

Yield: 10 servings

Ingredients

BBQ Chicken

  • 1800 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 3/4 cup BBQ sauce
  • 45 g tomato paste
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp Morton kosher salt
  • 1 Tbsp black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp smoked paprika

Blended Cream Sauce

  • 450 g 2% cottage cheese
  • Rendered juices from the baked BBQ chicken

Potatoes

  • 2250 g Yukon Gold potatoes, unpeeled and cut into chunks
  • 85 g bacon bits

Loaded Toppings

  • 1 large red onion, diced
  • 1 bunch green onions, diced
  • 227 g full-fat sharp cheddar, shredded

Instructions

  1. Mix the chicken thighs with the BBQ sauce, tomato paste, olive oil, salt, black pepper, and smoked paprika until coated. Pack tightly into 2 foil loaf pans. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour; longer is better.
  2. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Uncover the loaf pans, place them on a sheet pan, and bake 55 to 60 minutes. Rest the chicken in the pans for 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Remove the rendered juices from the pans and blend them with the cottage cheese to make the cream sauce.
  4. Meanwhile, add the potatoes to a 7 qt or larger pot and cover with cold water. Bring to a boil over high heat and boil 20 to 25 minutes until fork-tender. Drain, return to the pot, and mash. Mix the blended cream sauce and bacon bits into the mashed potatoes until it reaches the desired consistency.
  5. Spread the potatoes into a 15 x 11 x 3 inch foil roaster pan set on top of a sheet pan. Sprinkle with half of the red onion and green onions.
  6. Finely chop the chicken and spread it over the potatoes. Add the remaining onions, then the shredded cheddar. Bake for 20 minutes, then rest 10 minutes. Divide into 10 servings.

Notes

Equipment: 2 foil loaf pans, 1 medium foil roaster pan (15 x 11 x 3 inch), sheet pan, 7 qt or larger pot, blender.

After reheating, top with Greek yogurt or sour cream if desired (not included in nutrition).

To reheat, microwave from frozen for about 6 minutes, then let sit covered for a few minutes.

Entire casserole: 5800 calories, 550 g protein, 480 g carbohydrates, 190 g fat.
