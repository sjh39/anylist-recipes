Sour Green Apple and Grape Jelly Bar

Source: @officiallyemil

A tart, chewy jelly bar made from fresh Granny Smith apple juice, lime, and lemon set with beef gelatin around whole grapes. The juice is heated in the microwave, then poured over grapes in a 9x9 baking pan and chilled until firm. Cut into 9 squares.

Yield: 9 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh green apple juice, from 2-3 Granny Smith apples
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 4 Tbsp unflavored beef gelatin
  • 1 tsp citric acid (optional)
  • 2 cups green grapes

Instructions

  1. Arrange the grapes in your mold of choice, such as a 9x9 baking pan.
  2. Blend or juice 2-3 Granny Smith apples, then strain through cheesecloth to remove the pulp, yielding 1 cup juice.
  3. In a heat-safe bowl, combine the apple juice with the lime and lemon juice.
  4. Microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring between each, 2-3 times until hot.
  5. Add 4 Tbsp unflavored beef gelatin to the hot mixture and stir until completely dissolved.
  6. Add 1 tsp citric acid, if using, and mix well.
  7. Pour the mixture over the grapes.
  8. Refrigerate for 4-6 hours until set, then cut into 9 squares.

Notes

Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days for best freshness.
