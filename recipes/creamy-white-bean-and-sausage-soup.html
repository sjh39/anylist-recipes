Creamy White Bean and Sausage Soup

Source: @gatherednutrition

A creamy, high-protein, high-fiber soup with hot Italian chicken sausage, butter beans, and spinach. Two cans of beans thicken the soup while adding extra protein and fiber.

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1.5 lbs ground Italian chicken sausage (hot or mild)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken bone broth
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp dried Italian seasoning
  • Red pepper flakes, to taste
  • 2 (15 oz) cans butter beans (or any white bean), drained and rinsed
  • 1 pint heavy cream (use half for a brothier, less creamy soup)
  • 3 tbsp Parmesan, grated
  • 20 g Parmesan, grated, for serving
  • 2 cups fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped

Instructions

  1. Heat a large pot over medium heat with the olive oil. Add the onion and saute for 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic.
  2. Add the ground sausage and let it brown for 6 to 7 minutes, then stir to break it up.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, salt, black pepper, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes; cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the broth and bring to a boil.
  5. Stir in the beans and simmer for 3 minutes.
  6. Stir in the cream, 3 tbsp Parmesan, and spinach, and simmer for another 5 minutes to thicken.
  7. Adjust seasonings as needed and serve topped with the remaining Parmesan.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (USDA FoodData Central): 953 calories | 49 g protein | 71 g fat | 32 g carbohydrates | 7 g fiber
