Curry Udon with Beef and Egg

Japanese curry udon: thinly sliced beef and sweated onion simmered in a dashi, mirin, and soy broth thickened with curry roux, finished with ribbons of egg and spooned over chilled-rinsed udon. Keep a second 4-cube pack of curry roux on standby if you want it curry-er.

Servings: 2 servings

Ingredients

Curry Broth

  • 1 white onion, thinly sliced
  • 3/4 lb beef, thinly sliced
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 tsp dashi powder
  • 2 tsp mirin
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 4 Japanese curry roux cubes (one 4-cube pack)

Egg

  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 Tbsp water

Noodles

  • 2 blocks frozen udon noodles (Sanuki brand preferred)

Instructions

  1. Sweat the thinly sliced onion in a pot until cooked down.
  2. Add the beef and cook briefly, until mostly browned. Some red can still be showing; it is thinly sliced and will finish cooking in the broth.
  3. Add 6 cups water, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
  4. Add the dashi powder once the pot is at a simmer; do not boil the dashi powder.
  5. Add the mirin and soy sauce.
  6. Add the curry roux cubes and stir until completely dissolved. Add cubes from a second pack if you want a stronger curry flavor.
  7. Meanwhile, cook the frozen udon in a separate pot, then strain and rinse under cool water.
  8. Beat the eggs with 2 Tbsp water.
  9. Slowly stream the beaten eggs into the curry broth while stirring the pot slowly.
  10. Divide the udon between bowls, spoon the finished broth over the noodles, and serve.
