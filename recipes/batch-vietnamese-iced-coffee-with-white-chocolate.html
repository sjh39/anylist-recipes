Batch Vietnamese Iced Coffee with White Chocolate

A big-batch Vietnamese coffee brewed in four cycles through a 15 oz phin straight into a 64 oz jar of sweetened condensed milk, finished with white chocolate syrup. Makes about 12 to 14 cups depending on cup size, ice, and pour; calculated here as 13 servings. Keeps in the fridge for 3 to 4 days.

Yield: 13 servings

Ingredients

  • 760 g sweetened condensed milk (2 cans)
  • 240 g Cafe Du Monde ground coffee
  • 1200 g hot water, for brewing
  • 100 g white chocolate syrup
  • 200 g water

Instructions

  1. Pour 760 g sweetened condensed milk into a 64 oz jar.
  2. Add 60 g ground coffee to a 15 oz phin and set it on top of the jar.
  3. Pour 300 g hot water into the phin and let it drip completely into the jar.
  4. Empty and clean the phin to keep the filtering smooth.
  5. Repeat the brewing cycle 3 more times, for 4 cycles total (240 g coffee and 1200 g hot water), cleaning the phin between each cycle.
  6. Add 100 g white chocolate syrup to the jar.
  7. Add 200 g water and stir until fully combined.
  8. Pour over ice to serve. Store covered in the fridge for 3 to 4 days.
