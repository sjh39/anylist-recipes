High-Protein Maple Chicken Breakfast Sandwiches

Freezer-friendly breakfast sandwiches with a baked spinach and cottage cheese egg layer, maple-smoked paprika chicken patties, and Swiss on English muffins. Makes 12 sandwiches; reheat from frozen in the air fryer, oven, or microwave.

Yield: 12 sandwiches

Ingredients

Egg Layer

  • 8 large eggs
  • 1 cup 2% cottage cheese
  • 2 packed cups fresh baby spinach
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Maple Chicken Patties

  • 2 lb ground chicken
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (optional)
  • 1 Tbsp neutral oil

Assembly

  • 12 English muffins, split
  • 12 slices Swiss cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a 9x13 pan with parchment and grease it.
  2. Blend the eggs, cottage cheese, spinach, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper until smooth.
  3. Pour into the prepared pan and bake 22 to 28 minutes, until the center is set and no longer jiggles.
  4. Cool 10 minutes, then cut 3 by 4 into 12 squares.
  5. Gently mix the ground chicken, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, 2 tsp kosher salt, maple syrup, and black pepper if using.
  6. Divide into 12 portions, about 2.7 oz each. With wet hands, press each into a thin patty about 4 inches wide, slightly wider than the muffin since it will shrink.
  7. Heat 1 Tbsp neutral oil in a skillet over medium heat. Pan fry the patties 4 to 5 minutes per side, until they reach 165°F inside. Keep the heat at medium so the maple does not scorch.
  8. Toast the English muffins if desired to help prevent sogginess.
  9. Assemble each sandwich: muffin bottom, chicken patty, Swiss slice, egg square, muffin top.
  10. Cool the sandwiches completely, then wrap each individually in plastic wrap or foil. Refrigerate up to 4 days or freeze in a freezer bag up to 3 months.
  11. Reheat from frozen in the air fryer: unwrap and cook at 325°F for 5 minutes, flipping halfway through.
  12. Reheat from frozen in the oven or toaster oven: unwrap and bake at 350°F for 10 to 15 minutes, flipping halfway through.
  13. Reheat from frozen in the microwave: remove the plastic wrap, wrap the sandwich in a damp towel, and microwave 45 seconds. Flip upside down and microwave another 30 seconds.

Notes

Reheating times are for sandwiches straight from the freezer.
