Will W’s Avgolemono

The best possible sick-day, homey chicken lemon soup. A little more involved than a basic chicken soup, but well worth it if you’re trying to kick a cold.

Yield: 4-6 servings

Total time: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 1/2 bag (1 lb bag) baby carrots, chopped
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 1 inch butter, cut from a stick
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced (or 2 Tbsp jarred minced garlic)
  • 1 pinch all-purpose flour
  • 6 cups chicken broth (store-bought or made with Better Than Bouillon)
  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup orzo
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, optional
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Dried Italian seasoning, to taste
  • Red pepper flakes, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat a large pot over medium heat for about 2 minutes. Add the butter and olive oil. If the butter starts to brown or smell a little burnt, that’s fine.
  2. Add the carrots, celery, and onion; they will cool the pot down. Cook for about 7 minutes, until softened.
  3. Add the minced garlic and stir, then stir in the pinch of flour. The flour thickens the soup slightly and gives it more body.
  4. Add the chicken broth and a small dash each of salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning. Stir. Exact amounts don’t matter yet; you’ll adjust at the end.
  5. Add the raw chicken breasts whole. Turn the heat to high and bring the whole pot to a boil.
  6. Once boiling, reduce the heat to medium so it holds at a gentle simmer with small bubbles. Set the lid partly ajar to let heat escape and simmer for 15 minutes.
  7. Stir in the orzo and simmer the same way for 10 more minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. The surface will turn cloudy with white foam as the chicken proteins break down. This is normal; skim it off with a spoon and discard.
  9. Using tongs or a fork, remove the chicken, cut it into bite-size pieces, and return it to the pot.
  10. Stir in the juice of the 2 lemons. Season to taste with more salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if you like. Top with parsley if using.
  11. Serve with a big piece of crusty bread.
