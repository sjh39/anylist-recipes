Chicken and Egg Smash Salad

A creamy, high-protein chicken and egg salad smashed together with a blended cottage cheese sauce. Perfect for meal prep or a quick breakfast, lunch, or dinner. Serve on toast, in a sandwich, wrap, pita, or bagel, over baked potatoes, as a dip, or on its own.

Yield: 5 servings

Ingredients

Salad

  • 2 lb rotisserie chicken, shredded
  • 5 large eggs, hard-boiled and peeled
  • 1 bag green onions, chopped
  • 1/2 cup oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
  • Gherkins, chopped, as needed
  • Fresh dill, chopped
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan, grated

Sauce

  • 300 g low-fat cottage cheese
  • 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 4 Tbsp light mayonnaise (optional)
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • Garlic powder, to taste

Instructions

  1. Blend the cottage cheese until smooth.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the blended cottage cheese, Dijon mustard, light mayonnaise, honey, and garlic powder until well combined.
  3. Add the shredded chicken, hard-boiled eggs, green onions, sun-dried tomatoes, gherkins, dill, and Parmesan.
  4. Smash until well combined and creamy.
  5. Serve right away or refrigerate for meal prep.

Serving Ideas

  • Spread on buttered sourdough and top with chili crisp oil and furikake.
  • Fill a tortilla, wrap it up, and toast for a couple of minutes on both sides.

Nutrition
