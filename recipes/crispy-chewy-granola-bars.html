Crispy-Chewy Granola Bars

No-bake granola bars bound with peanut butter and honey, with rolled oats, puffed quinoa, and Rice Krispies for crunch, plus dried cranberries, pepitas, and mini chocolate chips. Pressed into pans, chilled, sliced, and kept refrigerated.

Yield: 24 bars

Ingredients

Binder

  • 2 cups runny creamy peanut butter
  • 1 1/3 cups honey
  • 2 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 1 heaping tsp sea salt

Mix-Ins

  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 cups puffed quinoa
  • 1 cup Rice Krispies cereal
  • 1/3 cup dried cranberries
  • 1/3 cup pepitas
  • 2/3 cup mini chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Stir together the peanut butter, honey, vanilla bean paste, and sea salt until smooth.
  2. Mix in the rolled oats, puffed quinoa, Rice Krispies, dried cranberries, pepitas, and mini chocolate chips until evenly coated.
  3. Press the mixture very firmly into parchment-lined pans.
  4. Chill at least 1 hour.
  5. Slice into 24 bars and keep refrigerated.

Notes

Can't find puffed quinoa or Rice Krispies? Use 5 cups rolled oats total instead of the 2 cups oats, 2 cups puffed quinoa, and 1 cup Rice Krispies (nutrition reflects the original version).
