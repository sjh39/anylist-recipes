Chicken Shawarma Rice Bake with Chickpeas

A one-pan oven meal prep: shawarma-spiced chicken thighs bake over jasmine rice, chickpeas, and vegetables in a 9x13 dish, then get a quick broil to char. Finished with spinach, lemon, feta, and parsley. Makes 4 meals.

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

Chicken and Rice

  • 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 185 g uncooked jasmine rice
  • 1 3/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon, juiced (divided)
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Shawarma Seasoning Blend

  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Add the 185 g uncooked jasmine rice, chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, bell pepper, red onion, garlic, chicken broth, and the juice of half the lemon to a 9x13 baking dish.
  2. Mix in half the shawarma seasoning blend, spreading everything evenly and keeping the rice submerged.
  3. Coat the whole chicken thighs with the remaining shawarma seasoning. Arrange them in a single layer on top of the rice mixture, keeping the tops exposed.
  4. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 40 to 50 minutes, until the rice is tender and the chicken reaches at least 165°F internally.
  5. Remove the foil and broil for 2 to 4 minutes to char the chicken, watching closely to prevent burning.
  6. Take the dish out of the oven. Remove the chicken and dice it.
  7. Add the spinach and the juice of the other lemon half to the hot rice and mix until the spinach is wilted.
  8. Finish with the diced chicken, crumbled feta, and fresh parsley. Divide into 4 meals.
