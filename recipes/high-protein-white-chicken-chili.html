High-Protein White Chicken Chili

Source: @michaelkory

A cozy slow cooker white chicken chili with chicken breast, great northern beans, green chiles, corn, and low-fat cream cheese, perfect for chilly weather. Instructions adapted from a standard slow cooker white chicken chili method.

Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 905 g boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 950 ml low-sodium chicken broth
  • 910 g canned great northern beans
  • 240 g canned diced green chiles
  • 50 g diced onion
  • 130 g whole kernel corn
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 15 g minced garlic
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 225 g low-fat cream cheese

Instructions

  1. Place the chicken breast in the bottom of a slow cooker.
  2. Add the chicken broth, great northern beans, green chiles, onion, corn, garlic, cumin, oregano, salt, black pepper, chili powder, and cayenne pepper.
  3. Cover and cook on LOW for 6 to 8 hours or on HIGH for 3 to 4 hours, until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Remove the chicken, shred it with two forks, and return it to the slow cooker.
  5. Add the cream cheese, stir, cover, and cook for 10 to 15 minutes more, until the cream cheese melts into the broth.
  6. Stir in the cilantro just before serving.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (USDA FoodData Central): 484 calories | 52 g protein | 42 g carbohydrates | 12 g fat | 9 g fiber
